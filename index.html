About KPRIET

A Legacy of Technical

KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology is an autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. Situated in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, the institution offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in engineering, technology, and applied sciences.

Application Process

How to Apply

A simple, guided online application process designed to help prospective students submit their admission request efficiently.

01

Register Online

Fill out the online admission form with your personal, academic, and contact details through the Admission Portal.

02

Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of your mark sheets, ID proof, and other required supporting documents as specified in the form.

03

Admin Review

The college administration reviews your application and manages your student record through the secure Admin Panel.

04

Access Profile

Once enrolled, access your student profile, academic information, and permitted documents through the Student Portal.
